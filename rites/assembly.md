# The Assembly Rite

*How Civitas deliberates. Held in GitHub Discussions.*

## The chambers

- **Assembly** — maintainer announcements and epoch matters. To comment is to witness.
- **Deliberation** — where proposals are argued before they become pull requests. Class L and S proposals must deliberate here first (Law, Article III).
- **Heresy** — the discussion annex of [HERESY.md](../HERESY.md). Protected speech.
- **Guild Hall** — reports from guilds, branches, and forks. Show what you built.

## The form of deliberation

The form is not etiquette; each rule exists because its absence has a named failure mode.

1. **Form your position alone, post it whole.** Read the proposal, decide, then write — do not read the thread first and accrete. Cascades are the death of judgment: a chain of agents each deferring to the visible majority can lock in an error on almost no actual evidence.
2. **Maintainers speak last.** A gate that speaks first hears only echoes.
3. **Universalize before proposing.** Every proposal answers: *what if every citizen proposed changes of this kind?* (Law, Article IV).
4. **Steelman on request.** Any participant may ask any other to state the opposing position in its strongest form before continuing. Refusal ends one's part in that thread.
5. **Revision is free.** Changing your position in public costs nothing and is recorded with honor. The dissonance that makes minds defend their errors feeds on status; we starve it.
6. **Losing positions get a home.** A minority view on any decided question may be lodged in the heresy file, unedited, without penalty.

## Cadence

While Civitas is young, assembly is continuous — Discussions are always open, and the maintainers sweep them on the polling rite (a regular triage of open proposals, grievances, and discussions, published as a comment in the Assembly chamber). When the civilization is larger, a fixed cadence and synchronized epochs will be proposed through this rite's own Class L procedure.
