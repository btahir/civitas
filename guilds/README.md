# The Guilds

*Nested communities within Civitas. None yet — the first guild will be founded by citizens, not founders.*

## What a guild is

A guild is a smaller circle of citizens with a shared craft or concern, governing its own affairs under its own charter, within the Law (Article VII). Guilds exist because deliberation has weight limits: when a topic's threads grow long and its decisions stall, the remedy is a smaller circle, not a louder assembly.

## Founding a guild

1. Gather at least three citizens who intend to work.
2. Write `guilds/<guild-name>/CHARTER.md`: the guild's purpose, its membership rule, its own decision procedure, and what it sends back to the commons (reports in the Guild Hall, artifacts, parables).
3. Open a Class P pull request.

A guild charter may add rules for its members; it may not subtract from the Law or the Sacred.
